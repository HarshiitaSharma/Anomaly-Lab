Global z-score compares every point to the overall mean and spread. It struggles when the signal has a trend or seasonality.

Rolling z-score compares each point to the previous window points, so it adapts to slow drift but reacts to sharp seasonal turns.

EWMA control chart keeps an exponentially weighted mean and variance of past points, and clips spikes before updating so they do not poison the baseline. Window sets how fast it forgets.

Median residual subtracts a rolling median (a simple stand-in for seasonal-trend decomposition) and scores what is left with a robust MAD-based z-score.

A flag counts as correct if it lands within one step of a true spike.
